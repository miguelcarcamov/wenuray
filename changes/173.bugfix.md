`envs/wenuray-cuda11.yml` installs CUDA 11.8, the version its CuPy 13 is built against, instead of 11.4; the two mixed in one process and cuBLAS failed (`CUBLAS_STATUS_INVALID_VALUE`). Its minimum driver is now R520. Wenuray also runs Pyralysis with the conda environment's CUDA (`CUDA_PATH`), so a system CUDA on the host can't mix its libraries in.

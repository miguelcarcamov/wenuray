Solints from the data (#66). `solint="auto"` takes the shortest solint whose predicted solution S/N reaches twice minsnr, resolved when the round starts against the model the last accepted round left. Its length avoids scans ending in a solution too short to reach minsnr, which calflag would flag. `Repeat(Phasecal("auto"))` (`type = "repeat"` in a recipe) runs a step again at shorter solints, coming down step by step: each round's solint is at least `1 - max_shortening` (half, by default) and at most `1 - min_shortening` (3/4) of the last, so each round's model is better for the next, shorter solint, and a repeat with no step of its class before it starts at a whole scan. On ALMA Band 7 data, halving the solint down to one integration left the residual at 1.1 x thermal, where jumping from a scan straight to one integration left it at 1.5 x; `max_shortening=1` lets each round take the shortest solint the data support. It stops when the gains are consistent with their noise, the data support no such solint, or a round is rejected; the run then goes on with the next entry. Each round records the solint it resolved to in its `solint`, and its step reads e.g. `Phasecal('auto' -> '180s')`. A repeat after a step of the same class, e.g. `Phasecal("inf"), Repeat(Phasecal("auto"))`, starts only at a solint shorter than that step's.

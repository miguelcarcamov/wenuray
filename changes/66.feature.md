Solints from the data (#66). `solint="auto"` takes the shortest solint whose predicted solution S/N reaches twice minsnr, resolved when the round starts against the model the last accepted round left. Its length avoids scans ending in a solution too short to reach minsnr, which calflag would flag. `Repeat(Phasecal("auto"))` (`type = "repeat"` in a recipe) runs a step again at shorter solints, each at least `min_shortening` (25%) shorter than the last. It stops when the gains are consistent with their noise, the data support no such solint, or a round is rejected; the run then goes on with the next entry. Each round records the solint it resolved to, e.g. `Phasecal('auto' -> '180s')`.

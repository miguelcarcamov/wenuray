`wenuray run` tells what it is doing: each phase (resetting the data, checking it, solving, applying, imaging) as it starts and how long it took, and Pyralysis's iterations as it works, instead of nothing until the round ends. Log lines carry their time; `--debug` adds the details, e.g. all of Pyralysis's output. Observers get the phases through a new hook, `SelfcalObserver.on_phase`.

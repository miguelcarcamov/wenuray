The log reads as a trace of the run: each round ends with a block of its solutions (how many failed minsnr and on which antennas, intervals with too few antennas to determine the gains), its gains against their noise ("a correction" or "consistent with noise", judged on as many solutions as the table has), its image against the thermal noise, and its decision; a round rejected for lack of S/N suggests a longer solint or combined spectral windows. The run ends with a table of every round. `selfcal.toml` records each round's solutions, and `wenuray run` reports steps accepted, not rounds.
